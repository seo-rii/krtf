This source is fictional AI-authored gold, not a human-verified or sampled
workplace corpus. All source documents, glossary facts, target positions and
identity labels were fixed before resolver preparation or model generation.
No resolver, retrieval engine or generation model was called by the author.
The independent reviewer must read the final source and approve all 96 cases
before recording independent_review.json and pinning its exact byte hash.

Twelve new lexical families, eight independent fictional events per family.
All 96 documents are final confirmation; none are development/tuning records.
Three styles each have four families: incident investigation, procurement
email, and project handover/chat. Family independence is a designed synthetic
property; the recurring eight challenge structures are correlated and do not
constitute 96 independent real-world audit events.
A is consistently a domestic procedure and B a foreign system, and positive
shared-alias cases select B. These designed correlations limit generalization;
this corpus must not support an unconstrained operational safety claim.

Each family has two unrelated registered entities with separate canonical
names and Korean aliases, but one shared four-letter Latin alias. Each term's
country, operational stage, referent type and organizational scope are
explicit in its full definition. The two entities differ on all four axes.
The 60 novel lexical names were checked against 20 tracked fixture JSON/YAML
artifacts, including prior routing and evidence-efficiency source corpora;
source_exclusion.json preserves their exact paths and hashes. There were no
string overlaps.

Within every family the source-authored challenge positions are:
  1 LINK A: four explicit attributes in one paragraph.
  2 LINK B: the same target is followed across three paragraphs; all four
    attributes belong to that target rather than nearby unrelated objects.
  3 LINK A: exact canonical name and owning organization, with country and
    operational stage omitted. Omission is explicitly distinct from conflict.
  4 LINK B: the shared Latin alias is disambiguated by function and attributes.
  5 NIL: an independent foreign object (odd families), or a defunct unrelated
    old policy (even families), has the same alias; it is not a version of A.
  6 NIL: nearby attributes belong to B; the selected alias refers to a third,
    external object. Transferring B's attributes to it is an attribution error.
  7 NON_MENTION in odd families: a metalinguistic letter/string example.
    COMPOSITIONAL in even families: alias+담당자 means a human; core and full
    target spans are intentionally different.
  8 AMBIGUOUS: the shared Latin alias appears without distinguishing evidence.

The resulting labels are LINK48, NIL24, NON_MENTION6, COMPOSITIONAL6,
AMBIGUOUS12. The target always records exact Unicode codepoint core and full
spans. Role expressions are not relabeled as the underlying procedure.

source_support_annotations is source-side gold only. It gives literal support
spans with entity ownership, allowed missing attributes, and explicit mismatch
spans. It must not be passed as model input, used to manufacture retrieval
candidates, or used to select final confirmation methods. In negative case6,
all four support_units intentionally belong to B, not the selected external
alias. The annotations help diagnose what an oracle could recover without
pretending that a literal value proves semantic attribution.

Reviewer checklist:
  * Read full glossary definitions and all eight documents in every family.
  * Verify selected occurrence and full-surface identity independently.
  * Check especially omitted versus contradictory attributes, cross-paragraph
    anaphora, and the deliberately misleading attribution in case6.
  * Preserve NIL / NON_MENTION / COMPOSITIONAL / AMBIGUOUS distinctions.
  * Record all case IDs, author/reviewer separation, source hash, review-before-
    resolver and review-before-generation, human_verified=false, and unresolved
    disagreements=0 only after issues are resolved from source semantics.
