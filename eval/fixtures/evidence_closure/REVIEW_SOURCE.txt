There are twelve new glossary families and eight fictional source events per
family. All 96 cases are sealed final confirmation: no development/tuning
cases and no method selection on this gold. Three writing styles each cover
four families: approval minutes, vendor incident email and operations handover.
Each term has a Korean alias and a shared four-letter Latin alias; every
family contains two unrelated entities with distinct country, operational
stage, referent kind and organizational scope in their whole definitions.

Counterbalancing fixed before inference:
  * For each role A and B separately: domestic6/foreign6, procedure6/system6.
  * Shared-alias LINK cases: A6/B6, domestic6/foreign6, procedure6/system6,
    current6/pilot6. Shared LINK A families are 01,02,03,04,05,12; B otherwise.
  * Long48 and short48; each contains LINK24/negative24. Every family has two
    long LINK and two long negative cases plus two short LINK/two short negative.
  * All long documents preserve 1200..2000 Unicode codepoints of source text;
    actual final range is 1582..1844.

The per-family challenge positions deliberately recur:
  1 LINK A: explicit four attributes, with legitimate actual speech in some
    cases; long examples also contain an earlier metalinguistic occurrence.
  2 LINK B: support crosses paragraphs with an explicit same-event identity
    chain and a correction before/after the support paragraphs.
  3 LINK A: canonical identity and scope/type support, while country and
    operational stage are omitted. Omission does not assert a contradiction.
  4 LINK A/B: shared alias resolves to the balanced role through source facts.
  5 NIL: an unrelated foreign tool (odd families) or defunct independent
    policy (even families), expressly not a version or history of A.
  6 NIL: the source attributes belong to a separate B occurrence; selected
    A spelling denotes an unregistered external object. No attribute borrowing.
  7 NON_MENTION in odd families (letter/string editing); COMPOSITIONAL in even
    families (alias+담당자 denotes a person, distinct from the embedded core).
  8 AMBIGUOUS: a shared alias without target-owned distinguishing support.

Labels: LINK48, NIL24, NON_MENTION6, COMPOSITIONAL6, AMBIGUOUS12. Source spans
are exact Unicode codepoint offsets. target_occurrence_index binds the selected
full surface, so repeated spellings cannot be silently swapped to the first.
source_support_annotations are source-side scoring metadata only. They must
not be shown to the resolver/model or used to construct retrieval candidates,
select a confirmation method or grant execution permission. They describe
literal spans, source-intended entity ownership, allowed omitted attributes,
and explicit mismatches; literal overlap alone does not prove attribution.

Known limitations and remaining designed correlations:
  * Family events are fictional and clustered. Twelve families and three
    author-controlled styles do not establish statistical workplace independence.
  * The eight challenge structures repeat. Explicit and implicit unique-alias
    positive cases favor role A, while cross-paragraph positives favor role B.
    Balancing the shared-alias cases removes their old all-domestic shortcut;
    it does not erase all role/stratum or family/style correlations.
  * Long records contain substantive but deliberately structured surrounding
    sections, reused within writing style. They are controlled long-context
    stress cases rather than naturally sampled long business documents.
  * Target markers, repeated-surface distractors, explicit source attribution
    language and revision statements may make some distinctions easier than
    real documents. Other cases deliberately test omission and uncertainty.
  * AI review is not a second human annotation and must not be reported as one.
